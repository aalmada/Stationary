Free rides, structured workouts, training plans, and an FTP ramp test. Power and resistance controls appear when your machine supports them.

A scene driven by you

Open the optional 3D ride scene, including on a second display. Cadence, heart rate, and effort bring the track to life.

Bring your bike

Use a Bluetooth Fitness Machine Service (FTMS)-compatible bike or trainer, optionally with a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor. A heart-rate sensor alone supports free rides. Structured power workouts and the FTP ramp test require a machine with power control; heart-rate-guided workouts also require a heart-rate sensor.

Not every Bluetooth fitness device supports FTMS or every control. For power-reporting bikes, speed and distance are estimates based on power.

Safety: workout targets are guidance, not medical advice. Heart-rate limits are not medical monitoring. Some trainers can retain resistance after an abrupt stop. Follow your equipment's safety instructions.
